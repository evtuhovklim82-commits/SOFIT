# SOFIT 4.0 Professional and GRAFIS 26

The baseline is SOFIT 3.0 at `5901543`. The comparison uses GRAFIS vendor documentation and the SOFIT source and tests. The GRAFIS application and production hardware were outside the verification setup. The table describes coverage by workflow.

| Workflow | GRAFIS documentation | SOFIT 3.0 implementation | SOFIT 4.0 and remaining gaps |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Interactive constructions and library | [Interactive interface](https://help.grafis.de/en-gb/Content/Interaktive_Konstruktionen-Basiswissen/Interaktive_Oberflaeche.htm), [library configuration](https://help.grafis.de/en-gb/Content/Module/Die_Gestaltung_der_Holenliste.htm) | A built-in template wizard creates formula-driven points, edges and parts. The parameter editor and replay engine recalculate named geometry and supported modeling operations. See `TemplateWizard.cpp`, `ParametricDraft.cpp` and `ModelingHistory.cpp`. | New rounded patch pocket in the template wizard. Live construction handles, custom library modules with bindings, shape presets and general downstream dependency editing remain open. |
| Curves, darts and panel lines | [New in GRAFIS 26](https://help.grafis.de/en-gb/Content/Neues_in_GRAFIS_26/Neues_in_G26.htm), [construction tool categories](https://help.grafis.de/en-gb/Content/Tools/Werkzeuge.htm) | Editable quadratic controls on free geometry, split and join, straight cuts and relief seams, yokes, slash-and-spread, pleats, gathering and constrained dart transfer. Supported operations on draft geometry replay by size. See `Modeling.cpp`, `ContourModeling.cpp` and `ModelingHistory.cpp`. | An independent quadratic guide can be copied with position and control-point offsets and reversed direction. General curve hooking and tangent controls, curved relief and yoke cuts, and additional dart constructions remain open. |
| Sleeves, collars and pockets | [Interactive sleeve](https://help.grafis.de/en-gb/Content/Interaktive_Konstruktionen/Interaktiver_Aermel_90.htm), [pocket and sleeve options](https://help.grafis.de/en-gb/Content/Neues_in_GRAFIS_23/Neues_in_interaktiven_Konstruktionen.htm) | Set-in, wide-hem and gathered puff sleeve variants, linked seam checks, flat collar, straight stand and facing. | Workspace and file exchange | [GRAFIS interface](https://help.grafis.de/en-gb/Content/Grundlegendes/Grafis-Oberflaeche.htm), [vendor shortcuts](https://help.grafis.de/en-gb/Content/Grundlegendes/Wichtige_Regeln_fuer_Arbeit_mit_Grafis.htm) | Workflow menus, panels, shortcut customization, DXF preview and import/export, print PDFs, and native project recovery. ## Verification

- `ProjectPersistenceTest.cpp` opens legacy fixtures and verifies retention of product information and marker clearance in schema 15.
- `ProfessionalModelingTest.cpp` checks usable pocket geometry, graded dimensions, and project, DXF and PDF output.
- `ProductionTest.cpp` checks clearance at the boundary, automatic placement, and cutting report validity.
- `PatternWorkflowTest.cpp` exercises product editing, undo/redo, PDF export, curve copying, clipboard exchange and saving marker settings through the desktop UI.
- The GitHub Actions Windows workflow builds with MSVC 2022 and Qt 6.8.3, runs CTest and the GUI at 150% scaling, launches the extracted portable ZIP, and installs, launches and uninstalls via Inno Setup. Workflow runs identify the tested commit.
